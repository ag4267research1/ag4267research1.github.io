Our proposal to [ACCESS](https://access-ci.org/) — the NSF-funded national cyberinfrastructure program — has been accepted. The project, "Quantum-inspired GPU-accelerated Algorithms for Scientific Computing" (MTH260091), is awarded 200,000 ACCESS Credits for Aug 11, 2026 – Aug 10, 2027.

I am Co-PI on the project, with my advisor Dr. Mohammadhossein Mohammadisiahroudi (UMBC) as PI.
